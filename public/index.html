How to generate App Password? ↗
Generated from Google Account → Security → 2-Step Verification → App Passwords. Spaces are automatically stripped.

Google App Password Quick Setup:

  1. Go to your Google Account (myaccount.google.com).
  2. Click Security on the left navigation panel.
  3. Under "How you sign in to Google", ensure 2-Step Verification is ON.
  4. Search or click on App Passwords.
  5. Type an app name (e.g. sendAutoMail) and click Create.
  6. Copy the generated 16-character code and paste it into the password field above.
